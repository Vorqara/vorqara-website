Proposed refined concept | v1.0

TAGLINE
Security and infrastructure, connected.

CONCEPT
A crisp, connected V mark built from two tapered arms in blue and cyan. The split colour treatment communicates connected security and infrastructure while staying close to the supplied brand kit's blue/cyan direction. The wordmark is uppercase, widely spaced, and designed for enterprise contexts.

LOGO USAGE
- Use horizontal logo on website headers and wide layouts.
- Use stacked logo for compact promotional layouts.
- Use the symbol alone for social profile avatars and small app placements.
- Use dark-background logo on dark surfaces and light-background logo on light surfaces.
- Keep original aspect ratio; do not stretch, skew, add outlines, or apply unapproved effects.
- Leave clear space around the logo at least equal to the width of one symbol arm.
- Avoid placing the full logo over busy photography or low-contrast backgrounds.
- The tagline is optional; omit it where it becomes too small to read.
